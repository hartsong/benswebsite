Buttons (to put it in your website, do btn-# in the id)

Be sure to add the class: btn-block, btn-color-*insert a color here(only do common colors: red, blue, green, white, black, grey, you can't mix them.). To get background color, make the class btn-background-* insert color here(also common colors.). You can also use rounded corners by adding the class btn-rounded_10%, 20%, 30%, 50%, 100%.
